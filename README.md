### 🦾 sEMG-Controlled Prosthetic Hand — Bachelor Thesis (2025)

A low-cost, 3D-printed bionic hand controlled by the muscle signals of the forearm (sEMG). Tested on 10 users: **100 %** grip detection, **98.25 %** finger-movement detection, **5 kg** load capacity, about **5,000 EGP** total cost.

### 🚗 Autonomous Path-Tracking Vehicle (2026)

### 🚦 Traffic-Light Control with Reinforcement Learning (2026)

<p><img src="Reinforcement%20Learning/images/dqn-demo.gif" width="260"></p>

AI agents (Q-learning, SARSA and a Deep Q-Network) that learn when to switch traffic lights. The best agent cut the average waiting time by **47 %** compared with a normal fixed-timer traffic light.

**[→ See the project](Reinforcement%20Learning/)**

### 👁️ Vision-Based Lane Keeping (2026)

<p><img src="Image%20Processing/images/5-result.jpg" width="420"></p>

A camera-based lane detector built with OpenCV and ROS 2. It runs at **20.8 frames per second** on a Raspberry Pi and steers a 3D-printed scale car.
