### sEMG-Controlled Prosthetic Hand — Bachelor Thesis (2025)

<p>
  <img src="sEMG-Controlled%20Tendon-Driven%20Prosthetic%20Hand%20%28Bachelor%20Thesis%29/images/hand.jpg" width="23%">
  <img src="sEMG-Controlled%20Tendon-Driven%20Prosthetic%20Hand%20%28Bachelor%20Thesis%29/images/grip-bottle.jpg" width="23%">
  <img src="sEMG-Controlled%20Tendon-Driven%20Prosthetic%20Hand%20%28Bachelor%20Thesis%29/images/grip-phone.jpg" width="23%">
  <img src="sEMG-Controlled%20Tendon-Driven%20Prosthetic%20Hand%20%28Bachelor%20Thesis%29/images/grip-scissors.jpg" width="23%">
</p>

Low-cost, 3D-printed, tendon-driven bionic hand controlled by forearm surface electromyography (sEMG). Validated on 10 test subjects with **100%** grip and wrist-flexion detection and **98.25%** individual finger-flexion detection; carries **5 kg** at a total cost of about **5,000 EGP (≈ US$100)**. Thesis grade: **A+**.
